Subscription link

Copy this address, then paste it into your calendar app using the steps below.

Google Calendar

  1. Open Google Calendar on a computer.
  2. Next to Other calendars in the left column, click + then From URL.
  3. Paste the address above and click Add calendar.

Outlook

  1. In Outlook on the web, open Calendar.
  2. Choose Add calendar then Subscribe from web.
  3. Paste the address, give it a name, and save.

Apple Calendar

  1. Choose File then New Calendar Subscription.
  2. Paste the address and choose how often it should refresh.

One-off download

Prefer a snapshot instead of a live subscription? This downloads the current season as a single file you can import. It will not update when titles change.
